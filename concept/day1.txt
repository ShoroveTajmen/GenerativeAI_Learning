Deep learning divides into 3 major options:
1. ANN (Artificial Neural Network) - Used for general pattern recognition, classification, and regression tasks on structured/tabular data.

2. CNN (Convolutional Neural Network) - Used for image and video recognition, object detection, and processing grid-like data such as images.

3. RNN (Recurrent Neural Network) - Used for sequential data such as time series, natural language processing, and speech recognition.

4. RL (Reinforcement Learning) - Used for training agents to make sequences of decisions by rewarding desired behaviors, commonly applied in robotics, game playing, and autonomous systems.

5. GAN (Generative Adversarial Network) - Used for generating new data samples that resemble a given dataset, commonly applied in image synthesis, data augmentation, and creative tasks such as art and music generation.
